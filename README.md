# Football Modeling

American Football is a very popular sporting event in both the United States and Canada. The game has evolved from a sport of physicality to a cerebral sport, often requiring players to memorize massive playbooks which can be upwards of 200 pages. Through the years, pro football players have improved to a point where their physical capabilities are not different enough to decide wins. The game has shifted to where wins are more often decided by strategy and the prediction of the opposing team’s plays. With the advent of machine learning models, we believe there is an untapped potential for tools that can assist coordinators in making decisions within collegiate and professional football. 


# Our Mission

The ultimate mission of this project is to influence the current strategy of how to counter offenses in collegiate and professional football by providing a resource that will shatter how defensive coaches view how offenses call plays. Using machine learning we will try to accurately predict every offensive play given any situation possible at a high success rate. This will give defenses a major advantage by eliminating hundreds of different alternative play calls an offense may call.
